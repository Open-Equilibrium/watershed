# Code of Conduct

Watershed follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) ([translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations)).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, focus on what is best for the community, and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Harassment or other unacceptable behavior as defined by the Covenant is not tolerated in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions, decision records) or when representing the project elsewhere.

**Enforcement contact:** b-weber@gmx.at. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially on a best-effort basis. Enforcement follows the Covenant's community-impact guidelines (correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban).
