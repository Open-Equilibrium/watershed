  • The range follows the list: its two handles mark the steps the list shows and move as it scrolls; dragging the range scrolls the list. Dragging a handle limits the list to the steps in the range, named by the "Range" chip; its ✕, a double-click on a handle or Esc clears it. The handles replace "drag to focus"; scrolling still zooms.
  • Jump: a click on the timeline outside the range jumps to the step at that point, selects it and moves the range there. A click on a node in the session tree jumps to its step too, expanding its turn.
  • Filters: the Filter button or ⌘F opens a row: kind of step, the session's tool names, failed only, and text. Steps that do not match draw faint in the list and the timeline but keep their place; ↑↓ then move between matches only.

  • Compact: 200 instead of 300 px wide, 22 px rows, turns as "T1", a response's first words instead of "Message", long text shortened with a tooltip.
  • Collapsible: a button collapses the tree to a rail with one dot per turn; below a 1280 px window it starts collapsed, which leaves the context track room at 1050 px.
  • Nodes by kind: shape and color match the list's Kind tags: a ring for a prompt (larger, so turns stand out), a dot for a response, a square for a tool, a diamond for an approval. A failed tool has a red outline and the selected step a blue one. Shape carries the kind, so it never depends on color alone.
  • Branches without colors: lines are gray and only the shown branch is blue; the branch letters in the tree, the chat and Compare are gray, the shown one blue.
