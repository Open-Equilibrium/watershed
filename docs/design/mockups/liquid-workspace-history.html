Liquid Workspace: History and Roles

Mockups for the M3a slice in PLAN.md, approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-01: one History over the Workspace, revert and Restore version, allow-only Roles with protected Blocks, and Workspace settings. They follow the V-Spec, ADR-0235 and guidelines.md; the shell is the one in the Pages mockups.

1 · History

"History" at the Explorer's foot opens a dialog over the current Page or Source; closing it shows that Page again. It lists every Action of the Workspace, newest first: who made it, from where, and what it changed. Filters narrow it to a Page or Block, an actor or an origin; "Page history" and "Block history" open it already filtered. Typing is grouped into one entry per pause. ⌘Z reverts your last Action and appears as an Undo entry. The selected entry shows each object it changed and a preview of the change.

2 · Revert

Revert records a new Action that undoes the selected one, as git revert does; the original stays in History (ADR-0235). Reverting the deletion brings back the Block, its Views, Connections and Automation, and turns the Workspace Automation on again.

3 · Restore version

The clock in the Page header opens "Page history" as a dialog, as Notion does. It shows the Page's versions: save points, plus one before each change made by an Automation or by someone else. Choosing one previews the Page as it was, with what changed since marked. Restore version returns the whole Page, also when later changes block a revert, and is itself one Action. A Block that other Pages show changes there too, and the banner says so.

4 · Roles

Settings opens from the Explorer's foot or with ⌘, as a dialog over the current Page, like Notion's Settings; Roles is one of its sections. A Role allows capabilities on areas, Pages, Blocks and Sources; anything it does not list is denied. Rights inherit from an area to everything in it, and the table shows where a right comes from. Three editable templates follow Notion's levels: Can view, Can edit and Full access. In M3a a Role is assigned to Automations; people join with sync and agent profiles with agents (ADR-0235).

5 · Protecting a Block

"Protect…" in a Block's menu, also for a Page or a Source, stops it from inheriting rights, so only the Roles allowed on it may change it (ADR-0235); it needs Manage. Reading still follows the area. Protection controls who may change it in Liquid, not who holds it: in M3 every authorized device holds the complete Workspace, so content that must stay off other people's devices belongs in a separate Workspace, as the dialog says. A protected Block shows a lock in its header.

6 · Workspace settings

The Settings dialog keeps the approved App sections and adds a Workspace group: General, with the name and the areas, and Roles. Each area's menu in the Explorer also renames it.

Decisions

Decided (ADR-0235)

  1. History is a sequence of Actions plus save points; Revert records a new reversing Action; Restore version returns a Page to an earlier point, also when later changes conflict; ⌘Z appears as Undo.
  2. Roles stay allow-only; a protected Page, Block or Source stops inheriting rights; a Block's rights follow its home, not the Page showing a View; read access is never finer than an area, and in M3 the Workspace is the confidentiality boundary.
  3. M3a assigns Roles to Automations.

Approved with these mockups (2026-11-01)

  1. History is a list with a detail pane, like the Diff tab; typing groups into one entry per pause of a few seconds.
  2. Capabilities appear as the columns Read, Propose, Apply, Approve and Manage; templates are Can view (Read), Can edit (Read, Apply) and Full access (all).
  3. The person who creates a Workspace holds Full access, which a protected Block always keeps, so nothing can lock its owner out.
  4. Workspace settings: General with name and areas, and Roles.

Added on request, approved the same day

  1. History, Page history and Settings open as dialogs over the current Page or Source; closing one shows it again. Settings sits at the Explorer's foot beside Automations and History. This replaces the approved Settings page that took over the main area.
