Rules

  • Opening Settings: Liquid → Settings… (⌘,) in the menu bar. The page replaces the main area; ⌘, or esc closes it.
  • Tap means pressing and releasing Caps Lock within 0.6 s without another key (its limits). In another app it starts Liquid or brings it to the front. Inside Liquid a tap does nothing until the PowerBar exists; then it opens the PowerBar.
  • Hold for 0.6 s or more inside Liquid to show the badges. ⇪ + key runs the command at once, without waiting for the badges. Holding Caps Lock in another app does nothing.
  • ⇧⇪ turns Caps Lock on or off in every app while the Liquid key is on.
  • Profiles: Liquid key, Standard and Custom; users can switch at any time. Standard uses ⌘ only and leaves Caps Lock unchanged. Switching to Standard restores Caps Lock at once and removes the background item. Changing a shortcut in Liquid key or Standard starts a new Custom profile from it. Custom is kept while another profile is active, and its switch decides whether ⇪ combinations exist. "Reset all to defaults" drops every change and never turns the Liquid key on or off. The Liquid key needs macOS 13; when it cannot be turned on, the previous profile stays and Liquid says why.
  • Changing a shortcut: click the shortcut, or press ⏎ on its row, to record. Press the new keys; esc cancels. Changed shortcuts are outlined and offer a reset.
  • Conflicts: if another action in the same area already uses the keys, a notice appears. Only "Use anyway" takes them and clears the other action. The same keys may be used in different areas; for example, esc stops a run in the input area and rejects in an approval. Keys of a fixed row, macOS standard shortcuts such as ⌘Q, and keys that type where the action works are refused with a notice.
  • Liquid key bindings: every Liquid ⌘ shortcut becomes ⇪ + the same key, and its ⌘ variant keeps working. Platform standards stay ⌘ only: Settings… (⌘,), Quit, Close, Hide, Minimize, copy, paste, undo and text editing. In the Liquid key profile, holding ⌘ shows no badges; in Standard it still does.
  • Effect: the menu bar, the badges and the key bindings follow the settings at once. Tabs show a badge only for keys the menu bar cannot show, such as ⇪1.
  • First start: nothing is preselected, and both buttons carry the same weight; esc chooses Standard. Turning on the Liquid key may make macOS show a notice that a background item was added.
  • Saving: a JSON file in the app data folder holds the profile and the changed shortcuts.

  • When: after the new keys are recorded, and after "Use anyway" when they conflict, before anything changes. Only while Liquid key or Standard is active and the kept Custom profile differs from the profile it is based on: at least one changed shortcut, or its Liquid key switch differs. Otherwise nothing would be lost, and the new Custom profile replaces it without asking.
  • Place: below the recording row, where conflict notices appear; the row shows the pressed keys and is not changed yet. Recording stops while Liquid asks.
  • Text: the action, its keys and the active profile, then what is lost: "4 changed shortcuts", "1 changed shortcut", or "the Liquid key turned off" when only the switch differs.
  • Keys: Cancel is the default and has the focus; ⏎ and esc choose it, and so does leaving the row. esc here never closes Settings. Replace Custom needs a click, or Tab and then ⏎. Holding the reveal key shows ⏎ on Cancel.
  • Replace Custom: the change applies in a new Custom profile based on the active profile, which becomes active; the old Custom profile is gone.
  • Cancel: nothing changes. The active profile, its keys and the kept Custom profile stay as they were.
  • Not proposed: a third button, "Edit Custom instead", would apply the change to the kept Custom profile and lose nothing, but it also switches the profile, which can turn the Liquid key on or off as a side effect; the hint line points to that path instead.
