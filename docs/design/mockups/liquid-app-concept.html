Liquid: the app with Chats (concept, revised)

Revised after the maintainer's review of 2026-11-03, who approved the rail, Chats as Blocks, one way to open things beside each other, sections and filters in the chat list, and the Inbox, and asked for: the Inbox on top and including tasks and deadlines, T3 Code's Snooze and Settle, Templates made from arrangements, tabs with splits instead of a separate "Open beside", and Notion's scale for the whole app. Its sizes are Notion's, from tokens.css (UI text 14 px, Page title 40 px, headings 30, 24 and 20 px, reading width 708 px, 28 px rows). Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-03 as the app's structure; mockups for implementation follow per slice.

1 · Rail and tabs

The rail, top to bottom: the Workspace you are in, its initial on a colored square, which switches Workspaces as Slack's rail does; Inbox, first, since it holds what waits for you; Pages, the Explorer of Pages and Sources; Chats; after a divider Files and Media, the store's two later views (ADR-0228), shown faint until they exist. At the foot Settings (the gear) and you. Only the Inbox carries a count, of what needs you, amber while an agent waits; Chats shows at most a quiet dot while something is unread, and the list itself shows which, as WhatsApp does, so nothing is counted twice. Above the main area, each open Page or Chat is a tab, as in Notion's desktop app and VS Code; tabs return after a restart. Here a Page shows a Chat Block among its other Blocks.

2 · Chats: sections, filters, Snooze and Settle

With Chats chosen, the sidebar lists them as WhatsApp does, two lines each. Filters on top; Pinned, the person's own sections, then Recent by activity. As in T3 Code, a Chat's menu can Snooze it, so it leaves the list until a chosen time or a new message, and Settle it, so it moves to Settled at the foot, collapsed, until someone writes again.

3 · Tabs side by side

Opening things beside each other is the tabs' split, as in VS Code: drag a tab to the right edge, ⌥-click an entry in a list, or press "Chat" in a Page's header, which opens the Page's own Chat as a tab beside it. Each side has its own tabs; the divider moves; closing the last tab of a side ends the split.

4 · A Chat Block in Arrange mode, saved as a Template

A Chat is a Block: its tile keeps a fixed height and its messages scroll inside, the composer always visible. Arrange mode's bar offers Save as Template, which opens the Template at once as an editable copy of the Page: the person removes or changes headings, text, icons and tiles right there, so there are no checkboxes for what to keep. On the right, each Block that holds data says what happens when a Page is made from the Template: a Chat asks each time, starts a new Chat or always shows the same one; a Database starts empty, keeps its rows or shows the same Database; an Automation is copied, paused or left out. The dashed outline marks the Block whose options show.

5 · A new Page from a Template

"New Page from Template" (the area's +, ⇧⌘N's menu or the PowerBar) lists the Workspace's Templates. A Template with slots asks for them first, here only the Chat: an existing one, a new one with chosen people and agents, or none. The Page then opens arranged, with the Template's headings and icons.

6 · Inbox: everything that waits for you

The Inbox gathers what needs the person, wherever it lives: approvals and questions from agents, mentions and replies, direct messages from people, tasks assigned to you that are due soon, and, once calendars exist, events about to start. Tasks are rows of a Database as in Notion, with a person and a date; the Inbox finds them in any Database. Each item opens where it lives; Snooze and Settle work as in Chats. The list is ordered by when things are due: what blocks an agent now comes first, since an agent waits for it, then overdue, today, tomorrow and later, each by its time; items without a date, such as mentions, follow by arrival. The sidebar's views select from it: Waiting (all of it), Due (only items with a date, as an agenda), Mentions, Snoozed and Settled. The rail's badge turns amber while an approval waits.

7 · On a phone

The rail becomes the tab bar: Inbox, Pages, Chats and Search; the Workspace switcher sits in Pages' title.
