Liquid Workspace: Board, Calendar and grouping

Mockups for the next M3a step in PLAN.md: Board, Calendar, grouping and copying a Table's View. A View of a Table on a Page holds tabs, as Notion's database views do; each tab has its own name, layout (Table, Board or Calendar), group, sort and filter. Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-22. Notion is the reference for every label.

1 · Tabs, and Group, Sort and Filter

Under the View's own title, its tabs; ＋ adds one. Below the tabs, a line with the active tab's Group, Sort and Filter, and Search, which is never saved. Group splits a Table tab's rows into foldable groups, each with its option and count; rows without a value form the first group, as in Notion. A tab groups by a Select or Checkbox field. Group totals follow with Formulas and the total row.

2 · Adding a tab, and the tab's menu

＋ asks for the layout first and adds a tab named after it, ready to rename. Clicking the active tab again, or right-clicking any tab, opens its menu: Rename, Layout, which changes it later and keeps its sort and filter, Duplicate tab and Delete tab. A View's last tab has no Delete tab; ··· on the title line deletes the View, as today. Tabs drag to reorder.

3 · Board

A Board tab needs a group; it takes the Table's first Select field, and with none it offers to add one. Each option is a column in the option's order, and a column for rows without a value comes first once it holds a card. A card shows its first field as title and the other filled fields, except the grouped one. Dragging a card to another column sets that field; inside a column it moves the row when the tab has no sort, as dragging a row does in the Table. “New” at a column's foot adds a row with that value. Columns are 224 points wide and scroll sideways inside the View.

4 · A card opens its row

Clicking a card, in a Board or a Calendar, opens its row beside it: every field as a line, edited with the Table's own pickers; ··· holds Delete row. esc or a click outside closes it. It is a popover, not a Page: whether a row opens as a Page waits until it is decided whether one View may show several Tables (D-115).

5 · Calendar

A Calendar tab places each row on the day of a date field, the Table's first Date field unless the tab chooses another; that choice takes Group's place on the line, and the month's controls sit on its right. ‹ › and Today move the month, which is not saved, as search is not. The week starts on the person's locale's first day. A day shows three rows and “+2 more”, which opens that day's list. Hovering a day shows ＋, which adds a row with that date; dragging a row to another day changes its date. “No date · 1” lists rows without one, to drag onto a day.

6 · Choosing the layout when a View is made

The slash commands Table, Board and Calendar each make a new Table and a View with one tab of that layout, as Notion's do: Board with a Select field “Status” (Not started, In progress, Done), Calendar with a Date field “Date”. Existing Table… asks, after the Table, for the first tab's layout, or copies the tabs of one of that Table's Views once.

7 · Duplicate and Copy

··· on the View's title line gains Duplicate and Copy, as the approved Blocks mockup lists them. Duplicate makes a new, independent Table “Expenses (copy)” with all fields and rows, listed in the sidebar's Tables, and puts its View, with the same tabs, right below. Copy then ⌘V on a Page does the same at the caret; a chip right after the paste offers “Paste as View”, which instead adds a View of Expenses with the copied tabs. Other Blocks get Duplicate and Copy with the Block handles that select a whole Block (PLAN.md).

Decisions

Already decided

  1. Table, Board and Calendar show one Table (ADR-0244); every Table has its own document, and deleting its last View on a Page asks whether to delete the Table too (ADR-0309, built).
  2. Duplicate creates a new, independent Block; pasting a copied Block duplicates it; a View is always explicit (ADR-0235, Blocks mockup 2026-11-01).

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-21

  1. A View holds several tabs, as Notion's database views; the View keeps its own title, and each tab has its own name, layout, group, sort and filter. Every tab of a View shows the same Table; D-115 decides whether one View may show several.
  2. Group, Sort and Filter sit on their own line under the tabs.
  3. The layout is chosen when a tab or View is made and changes later in the tab's menu.
  4. Clicking a card opens its row as a popover; rows as Pages wait for D-115.
  5. Slash commands Board and Calendar make a ready Table, as Notion's do.

Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-22

  1. The word tab in the UI (“Delete tab”) and “View tab” in GLOSSARY.md, because View already names a Block's place on a Page; Notion says “view” for both.
  2. Group by a Select or Checkbox field, for Table and Board; empty group first; a folded group is part of the tab, for everyone, as in Notion (maintainer, 2026-11-22); the last used tab is remembered per device. Grouping by text, number or date follows when asked for.
  3. A Board card shows the first field and the other filled fields; which fields a card shows becomes a setting later.
  4. The Calendar shows a month only; a week follows when asked for. Dates stay days (ADR-0242).
  5. A View's last tab cannot be deleted from the tab's menu; ··· Delete View removes the View.
  6. Duplicate copies a Table with all its rows; Paste as View copies the View's tabs.
