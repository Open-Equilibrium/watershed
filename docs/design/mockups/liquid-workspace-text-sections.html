Liquid Workspace: Sections

Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-01; they replace the dropped groups idea. A Section is the Block of text: its Paragraphs are one rich text, and ⏎ ends a Paragraph and starts the next (ADR-0322). --- or a Block of another type ends a Section. In Arrange mode a Section is one tile, so text is arranged as a whole, while every other Block is its own tile. Shell and wording follow the Pages mockups.

Revised by ADR-0322: a Section owns its text, and Paragraphs that belong side by side go into Sections of their own; there are no columns inside a Section. Formatting marks are in the formatting mockup.

Revised by ADR-0239 and ADR-0241: the rail with Inbox and Settings, the Workspace switcher in the sidebar's top row, tabs side by side, Chats in place of agent sessions and Notion's scale are in the app's structure; this file keeps its slice's content, and the shell is redrawn as each slice is built.

1 · How Sections form

Hovering over text shows the Section's outline and, faintly, its Paragraphs. Here the first Section holds five Paragraphs and ends at ---; the second ends at the photo; the text after the photo starts a third. Nobody creates a Section: it follows from the Page's order.

2 · Splitting and joining Sections

--- and ⏎ between two Paragraphs splits their Section. ⌫ at the start of the Paragraph below a divider removes the divider and joins both Sections. It needs no dialog, and Undo reverts it. Inserting a Block of another type between Paragraphs also splits a Section, and deleting it joins them again.

Decisions

Approved (2026-11-01)

  1. Paragraph. A body text, heading, list item, to-do or quote; ⏎ ends it, as in Notion. A toggle stays formatting: the Paragraphs indented below it fold with it.
  2. Section owns its text (ADR-0322, superseding “layout only”). It is a Block like any other, and its Paragraphs are one rich text, not Blocks.
  3. Boundaries. A Section ends at ---, at any Block of another type and at the Page's start and end. --- right after a Block of another type stays plain text, since a boundary already exists. There is no decorative divider.
  4. Phone. On a phone everything stacks in Page order.

Approved later the same day

  1. Markdown import follows CommonMark. Only a thematic break becomes a Section boundary: ---, *** or ___ on a line of its own. --- directly under a line of text underlines a heading (a setext heading, like ===), so it becomes a Paragraph formatted as a heading and no boundary. A --- block at the very start is YAML front matter and becomes no boundary either; inside code and table rows --- stays text.
  2. Markdown export writes headings as # and ##, never underlined, and writes each Section boundary as --- with a blank line above, so a round trip never turns a boundary into a heading.
