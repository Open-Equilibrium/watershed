Liquid: the sidebar

For the Tables slice in PLAN.md. Sections fold, and Workspaces switch without a dropdown.

1 · Sections fold

A click on a section's name folds or unfolds it; the arrow shows which. Liquid remembers it on this device. The section that holds the open Page unfolds when that Page is opened from elsewhere.

2 · Switching Workspaces

Every Workspace shows as its icon, and the one you are in is marked. A hover or long press shows a Workspace's name; ⌘1 to ⌘9 switch to the first nine. With one Workspace there is nothing to switch, so only its icon and name show.
