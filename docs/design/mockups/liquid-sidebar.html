Liquid: the sidebar

For the Tables slice in PLAN.md, after ADR-0310.

1 · Sections fold

A click on a section's name folds or unfolds it; the arrow shows which. Liquid remembers it on this device. The section that holds the open Page unfolds when that Page is opened from elsewhere.

2 · Switching Workspaces

The sidebar's top row shows every Workspace side by side (ADR-0310): the one you are in with its icon and name, the others as icons, ＋ adds one. A hover or long press shows a Workspace's name; ⌘1 to ⌘9 switch to the first nine. With one Workspace, the row shows its icon and name, and ＋. ＋ makes a Workspace named “New Workspace” and opens its name for editing. ＋ in Tables makes a Table in the open Page's area, else in the first area.

Three Workspaces
Six Workspaces: the rest behind “+2”

3 · Naming a Workspace and choosing its icon

A click on the Workspace you are in opens its name and icon. Both belong to the Workspace, so every device shows them. The icon is an emoji, as a Page's will be, or the name's first letter on a color.
